# Class and Advancement Stat Guide

**Draft for future player guidance.** Stat formulas, starting values, equipment bonuses, and recommended point ratios are still being designed and tested. Class and advancement names marked “working name” may change. This guide describes the planned system, not the current test build.

## Current trial: read your skill's scaling

The Skills panel now identifies the stat used by each skill and shows an estimated damage or barrier value. The Stats panel compares your equipped skills before and after your draft allocation, including equipment and current mastery. Damage is estimated per hit; multi-hit totals assume every hit lands on one target. Enemy bonuses, marks and counters can change the final result.

In the current trial, Knight skills (including Paladin) and all Dwarf skills (including Gunner shots, bombs and artillery) use **STR** for damage. Physical Elf skills use **DEX**. Mage spells and Magic Archer path skills use **INT**. DEX does not currently add attack speed or critical chance. Gunner DEX scaling remains a future design question.

Barriers use the recipient's **Max HP**, including equipment. Your VIT can increase your own barrier; a party barrier uses each ally's own Max HP. INT does not directly increase barrier strength in the current trial. Stats do not change cooldowns or effect duration. Raising Max HP or MP does not refill the resource.

Skill mastery and stat allocation use separate point pools. Canceling a draft leaves your saved stats unchanged. These explanations describe trial rules; the planned directions below are not final point-allocation recommendations.

## How stats work

Your character has four stats:

| Stat | Planned role |
| --- | --- |
| **Strength (STR)** | Improves strength-based physical attacks, such as sword, shield, and axe skills. Some skills may also deal more posture damage. |
| **Dexterity (DEX)** | Improves precision-based physical attacks, such as bow and gun skills. Its possible effects on critical chance and attack speed are still being balanced. |
| **Intelligence (INT)** | Improves magic attacks and may increase maximum MP for characters who use magic. |
| **Vitality (VIT)** | Improves maximum HP. A contribution to defense is under consideration. |

HP is your health; VIT is the stat you can invest in to improve it. A character's level and equipment may also affect combat values. Exact formulas are not final.

You gain **4 stat points per level from level 2 through level 80**, for a total of **316 points**. Starting stats are separate. You may put all four points from a level into one stat, split them among stats, or save unspent points. Skill points are a separate pool; their current design target is 79 points by level 80.

Your starting class stays the same. At level 20 you can complete your first advancement and choose one of its two paths. Further advancements are planned at levels 40, 60, and 80. Reaching the level and completing the advancement are separate steps.

## Skills and resources

Physical skills use individual cooldowns and do not cost Stamina. Magic skills use MP and an individual cooldown. Basic attacks, blocks, dodges, and sprints do not spend a resource; basic attacks and dodges have no separate cooldown. Action animations and timing still apply.

A Magic Archer gains MP upon completing the first advancement at level 20. The starting physical skills remain physical. The current Paladin concept uses physical sword-and-shield skills with radiant visual effects. If actual magic skills are introduced later, their MP rules will be designed separately.

## Knight — sword and shield

### Steel Knight

- **Consider STR** to improve sword-and-shield attacks and posture pressure.
- **Consider VIT** when you want more HP and survivability.
- **Playstyle:** counterattacks, shield strikes, forward attacks, and moving area attacks.

### Paladin

- **Consider STR** for the current physical sword-and-shield attacks.
- **Consider VIT** for survivability while protecting yourself and allies.
- **Playstyle:** attack with sword and shield, apply oath marks, and add short protective effects. The mark is not required for a skill's basic attack.

Paladin magic, MP availability, and any INT scaling are not finalized. Radiant visuals alone do not make a skill magical.

## Dwarf — axe

### Steel Dwarf (working name)

- **Consider STR** for axe attacks, throws, and heavy strikes.
- **Consider VIT** for HP and closer-range survivability.
- **Playstyle:** combine heavy axe strikes, throwing paths, and posture pressure.

### Gunner

- **STR and DEX scaling is still under review.** Axe skills, gunshots, bombs, and artillery must remain useful after advancement; exact recommendations will follow testing.
- **Consider VIT** for more HP.
- **Playstyle:** choose independent gun, bomb, and artillery skills. No gun-and-bomb sequence is required.

The current concept keeps the axe as the equipped weapon and brings out a gun during gun skills. Equippable guns are a possible future expansion, not a confirmed feature.

## Elf Archer — bow

### Sharpshooter path (working name)

- **Consider DEX** for physical bow attacks and precision shots.
- **Consider VIT** for more HP.
- **Playstyle:** select precise single shots, moving volleys, or piercing attacks.

### Magic Archer (working name)

- **Consider INT** for magic arrows and MP-related growth.
- **DEX remains relevant to your physical bow skills.** The exact DEX/INT contribution to magic arrows is not final; do not treat any point split as mandatory.
- **Consider VIT** for more HP.
- **Playstyle:** combine physical bow skills with magic arrows and nature-based area control. Magic skills use MP; physical skills use cooldowns without Stamina.

## Mage — staff

### Pyro

- **Consider INT** for magic damage and MP-related growth.
- **Consider VIT** for more HP.
- **Playstyle:** fire at the first advancement, earth after the level 40 advancement, and larger area spells later.

### Cryo

- **Consider INT** for magic damage and MP-related growth.
- **Consider VIT** for more HP.
- **Playstyle:** cold at the first advancement, lightning after the level 40 advancement, and larger area spells later.

## Choosing and changing your build

These are directions, not mandatory builds. The best allocation depends on skill selection, equipment, encounter difficulty, and final balance. Exact point ratios are intentionally omitted until playtesting supports them.

The current design calls for a preview before confirming stat allocation. Newly earned points can be allocated without a reset ticket; a confirmed full stat reset at a hub uses a Stat Reset Ticket. Skill resets use a separate ticket. Availability and other details remain subject to design and testing.
